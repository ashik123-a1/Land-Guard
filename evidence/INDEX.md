## 2. Key Empirical Findings for Judges

### Machine Learning & Rules (`reports/independent_ml_validation.md`)
- **Canonical 16-Feature Split (`n=375`) & Seller-Disjoint Split (`n=400`, Regime A)**: Majority Baseline scores `65.07%` Accuracy / `0.7884` F1 (`131` FPs). Rule Engine, Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and XGBoost score `100.0%` (`1.0000`), which our audit proved is caused by noiseless synthetic scenario rules and 3 oracle CV/OCR flags in `canonical_records.json`.
- **Registry-Only 13-Feature Split (Regime B, No Oracle CV/OCR Flags)**: Rule Engine & Logistic Regression achieve **`74.93%` Accuracy | `100.0%` Precision | `61.48%` Recall | `0.7614` F1** (`150/244` registry frauds caught, `94` document-level frauds require CV/OCR); XGBoost achieves **`73.33%` Accuracy | `0.7863` F1 | `0.9132` PR-AUC**.
- **Leave-One-Fraud-Family-Out CV (Regime C, 8 Folds)**: Supervised ML models achieve only **`4.27%` mean recall** on unseen orthogonal fraud families (`0%` on 7/8 families), whereas the **Deterministic Statutory Rule Engine achieves `100.0%` recall across all 8 folds**.
- **Operational Coupling with CV/OCR Noise & Admin Friction (Regime D)**: Rule Engine & Logistic Regression achieve **`90.40%` Accuracy | `0.9289` F1** (`TP=235, FP=27, TN=104, FN=9`); Random Forest achieves **`88.80%` Accuracy | `0.9170` F1 | `0.9726` PR-AUC**; XGBoost achieves **`88.00%` Accuracy | `0.9087` F1 | `0.9711` PR-AUC**.

### Computer Vision: Signatures, Seals, Anomalies (`reports/vision_validation_report.md`)
- **Official Seal Detection (`n=600`)**: **`99.67%` Accuracy | `100.0%` Precision | `99.61%` Recall | `0.9980` F1** (`TP=511, FP=0, TN=87, FN=2`).
- **Signature Detection — Assisted Box (`n=600`)**: Improved from `69.00%` $\rightarrow$ **`98.00%` Accuracy | `95.90%` Precision | `100.0%` Recall | `0.9791` F1** (`TP=281, FP=12, TN=307, FN=0`; false positives reduced from `186` to `12`).
- **Signature Detection — Unassisted Quadrant (`n=600`)**: Improved from `37.83%` $\rightarrow$ **`65.33%` Accuracy | `76.26%` Precision | `37.72%` Recall | `0.5047` F1** (`TP=106, FP=33, TN=286, FN=175`).
- **Visual Anomaly Detection (`n=50` Altered, `n=600` Clean)**: **`0.0%` False Positive Rate on clean digital** (`0/40`), **`0.50%` FPR across all 600 clean images** (`3/600`, down from `10/600`), and **`100.0%` Recall on controlled physical paste-overs** (`50/50`, up from `32/50`).

### Document OCR Reality (`reports/ocr_repair_report.md`)
- **English Subset & Structured Field Match (`n=48`)**: Suppressing translucent red watermark bleed and fixing `deskew()` false rotation reduced **English Subset CER from `0.8868` $\rightarrow$ `0.6021`** (`-28.47 pp`) and increased **Exact Field Match Rate from `47.50%` (`38/80`) $\rightarrow$ `87.50%` (`70/80`)** (`+40.00 pp`).
- **Bengali Script Reality**: **`1.0000` CER** on Bengali text blocks because `rapidocr_onnxruntime` lacks Bengali Unicode weights (`U+0980..U+09FF`) and `tesseract.exe` (`+ ben.traineddata`) is not installed on the host. Documented transparently without fabrication.
